Pick any point in West Bengal. The engine fetches its rainfall, heat, soil, drainage, salinity, slope and frost profile, weighs them by AHP, scores every native species on a Species Suitability Index, and returns a planted layout with the date to plant.

      Air quality, carbon and compliance

      APTI values are literature-derived from Indian urban studies using the Singh and Rao method; PM10 is the indicative CPCB NAMP annual mean for the nearest monitoring city.

      Provenance: CPCB and NGT publish guidance documents and orders, not per-species databases. APTI, dust-capture class and wood density are compiled from peer-reviewed Indian studies and the CPCB greenbelt and dust-capturing species compilations; treat them as representative ranges and replace with project measurements where available.

      Method

      The engine follows the framework's sequential pipeline. A coordinate is projected from WGS 84 to UTM zone 45N (EPSG 32645). Environmental attributes are fetched by inverse-distance interpolation over a grid of station and survey control points covering all six agro-climatic zones of West Bengal, standing in for the IMD, Bhuvan and SRTM rasters the Python backend would query live. Land cover applies an urban heat island increment and a compaction factor; micro-topography shifts the ponding index and water table.

      Criteria and weights

      Each criterion receives a severity si on Saaty's 1 to 9 scale from the site attributes. The pairwise matrix takes aij from the rounded severity difference, so the judgments are discrete and the consistency check is real rather than trivially satisfied.

      A W = λmax W(1)
      CI = λmax − nn − 1(2)
      CR = CIRI, matrix rejected and renormalised when CR > 0.10(3)
      SSIj = Σi wi · xij, species selected when SSIj ≥ 0.85(4)

      The principal eigenvector is found by power iteration. Random Index values follow Saaty (n = 8: 1.41, n = 9: 1.45). Tolerance xij is 1 where a species' capacity meets the site demand and falls linearly by 1.5 times the shortfall otherwise; pH and salinity use range matches, and obligate mangroves are gated on a tidal regime. Ground covers are scored without the air criterion, which is redistributed across the other weights for that stratum.

      Layout and schedule

      Planting nodes lie on a 2 m offset grid. Trees are placed centre-out, largest crowns first, up to target counts proportional to SSI, with mature canopies separated by at least 0.5 m; shrubs fill gaps outside 55 percent of each tree's crown radius. Where the ponding index exceeds 0.55 on a non-tidal site, each tree is set on an earth mound lifting the root collar 1.2 m above the projected flood line, and DEM depressions are retained as monsoon basins. The planting window is the mean onset date minus 21 to 15 days.

      Data sources represented

      Indian Meteorological DepartmentGridded annual rainfall normals, monsoon onset isochrones, pre-monsoon maxima and winter minima.
      ISRO Bhuvan and NBSS&LUPSoil typology, baseline pH, hydraulic conductivity class, land use and land cover for UHI multipliers.
      SRTM 30 m DEMElevation, slope and depression detection for runoff pooling and mound design. Also drives the relief heights on the 3D map.
      CPCB NAMP and NCAPAnnual PM10 for monitored cities and the non-attainment list (Kolkata, Howrah, Barrackpore, Asansol, Durgapur, Raniganj, Haldia).
      Botanical literatureTolerance vectors, APTI ranges, wood density and mature dimensions for 130 native, naturalised and reference exotic species of Bengal.
      Chave et al. 2014, IPCC 2006Pan-tropical allometry, carbon fraction and root to shoot ratio for sequestration estimates.

      Limitations

      As the framework notes, gridded meteorological products run at 10 to 25 km and cannot resolve street-scale micro-climates shaped by building shadow and anthropogenic heat. The embedded dataset inherits that limit: treat the output as the data-driven starting palette for a site survey, not a substitute for one. The synthetic micro-DEM illustrates the layout logic; connecting a real survey DEM replaces it.
